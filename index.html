Abandoned,
Not Forgotten

Since 1966, NASA has left hundreds of objects on other worlds: landers, moon buggies, rovers, a helicopter, a family photograph, even two golf balls. Some still do science. Most went silent long ago.

Every one of them is still out there, right where it stopped. This is their story.
